# Community Notes: r/SkincareAddiction

**Date read:** October 16, 2026
**Posts read:** ~[40] across Hot, New, and Top (month), mixing flairs
**Source:** old.reddit.com/r/SkincareAddiction (public, no login)

## Candidate distinctions

### 1. Routine and product posts
Posts where someone shares their products and asks for feedback on their regimen.
- Example: a photo of a product lineup with the title *"[Routine Help] is my skincare routine good, bad, or too much?"*

### 2. Personal skin concern posts
Posts where someone shares their own face or skin issue and asks the community for opinions or help.
- Example: three selfies with the title *"I'm curious as to how old everyone thinks I am, and if any of these wrinkles could get fixed."*

### 3. News and regulation posts
Posts reporting current news or policy, especially around sunscreen filters and how U.S. regulation compares with other countries.
- Example: *"The first new sunscreen in 25 years is finally arriving in stores in the U.S."*

### 4. Open discussion on trends and marketing
Posts that start a community conversation about social media trends or product marketing, with thoughtful replies.
- Example: *"[Miscellaneous] What skincare trend did you stop following because it just wasn't worth it for you?"* (116 comments)

## Observations
- Types 1 and 2 overlap: a routine post often includes a skin concern, and a skin concern post often includes products. This is likely my hardest boundary.
- Types 3 and 4 seem rarer than 1 and 2 in what I read, so class imbalance is a risk when I collect 200 posts.
- Types 3 and 4 tend to cite outside information or draw on many people's experience, while 1 and 2 center on one person's situation.
